# Rest Cue Clarity Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Implement only the approved Low Energy / Bedtime distinction.
Hunger, feeding, item effects and all care/sleep rules remain unchanged.

**Architecture:** Add a read-only Rest detail derived from the existing Rest
cue, DNA recovery threshold and preferred bedtime. Both renderers keep the
existing cue priority and suppression, use a lightning bubble for energy and
the existing bulb for bedtime. A fixed 100x18 caption sits above the pet and
beside the bubble: energy current/goal plus bar, or preferred bedtime.

**Tech Stack:** Portable C++/Python, Pillow, pytest, Unity, ESP32/TFT/LittleFS.

## Contract

- Injury and food keep their current priority. No hunger counter or new toast.
- For an existing Rest cue, trusted time at/after preferred bedtime or before
  06:00 shows Bedtime. If both reasons apply, Bedtime wins.
- Otherwise show Low Energy with goal 30/35/40 from the existing DNA trait
  and hysteresis. Reading details never calls tired() or alters its latch.
- Unknown calendar never invents bedtime. Learned bedtime is respected.
- Suppress details wherever the current bubble is hidden: sleep, actions,
  panels, away, paused, dead or egg. Existing enum values/NVS stay intact.
- ES/EN labels, numeric goal/time, stable geometry above y=43; no Status edits.
- New energy art reuses the approved lightning icon and current blank bubble;
  original hunger/bedtime/mood assets stay byte-identical. No new framebuffer.
